1. The 60-second summary

Tacoma RV has a paradox most dealers would envy: your data is good, but it’s invisible. When our crawler read your listing pages the way an AI assistant reads them — plain page content, no JavaScript — all 40 sampled pages came back as an empty shell: no specs, no price, no VIN, no headline. A shopper’s browser runs the scripts and sees a full page; an AI reader often doesn’t. On top of that, four fields AI filters on — RV type, sleeping capacity, length, and per-unit location — were missing on all 14 units analyzed. The result: your site could prove the answer to only about 1 in 4 of the 26 shopper questions we tested. None of this is an inventory problem — it’s a visibility problem, and Fix #1 resolves most of it at once.

7. Top 5 fixes — ranked by lead impact

Fix 1 — Make listing pages readable without JavaScript + add Vehicle structured data

IMPACT: HIGHEST · unlocks all 40 sampled pages at once — one web-vendor job

Problem: Static HTML of every sampled VDP carries no specs, price, VIN, headline, or structured data. Crawlers and AI readers without JavaScript see an empty shell. This single fix raises readability, coverage, and overall together.

  1. Ask your vendor for server-side rendering (or pre-rendering) of /inventory/ pages: price, specs, VIN, and H1 in the raw HTML.
  2. Add Product/Vehicle schema with identity, offer, availability, and labeled specs.
  3. Use one clear year/make/model H1 per unit with consistent spec labels.

Fix 2 — Tag every unit with an explicit RV type

IMPACT: HIGH · unblocks all category filters (travel trailer / fifth wheel / toy hauler / Class)

Problem: 0 of 14 units analyzed state their RV type as data. Every “travel trailer under $X” and “fifth wheel under $Y” question fails on this one field.

  1. Add a type field to each unit: Travel Trailer, Fifth Wheel, Toy Hauler, Class A/B/C, etc.
  2. Show it as page text, not just a category breadcrumb.

Fix 3 — Add sleeps + length to every unit

IMPACT: HIGH · unlocks family + size questions

Problem: Both fields are 0 of 14. Two columns of factory data recover the two most-asked towable filters.

  1. Copy factory sleeps + length into spec fields: “Sleeps 6”, “Length 33 ft 11 in”.

Fix 4 — Put a per-unit location line on every listing

IMPACT: MEDIUM-HIGH · unlocks “near me” proof

Problem: 0 of 14 units state their location. AI never substitutes your city — each page must say “On the lot in Fife, WA” itself.

Fix 5 — Close the small gaps: 2 prices, 3 GVWRs, 4 slide counts

IMPACT: MEDIUM · finishes the fields you’re already strong on

Problem: Price missing on 2 category pages, GVWR on 3 units, slides on 4. An hour of cleanup takes four fields to near-100%.
