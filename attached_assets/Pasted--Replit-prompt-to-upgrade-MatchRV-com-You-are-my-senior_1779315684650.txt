# Replit prompt to upgrade MatchRV.com

You are my senior full-stack engineer, product designer, and UX implementer working on **MatchRV** (matchrv.com), an AI-powered RV marketplace.

The site is already live. Your job is to improve the UX, information architecture, homepage hierarchy, marketplace clarity, and trust signals while preserving existing business logic and core backend flows.

The current site over-emphasizes the Match Report questionnaire and under-emphasizes the fact that MatchRV is a real RV marketplace with live inventory.

The redesign goal is to make MatchRV feel like:

* a trustworthy RV marketplace,
* with real inventory,
* enhanced by AI-powered matching and shopping tools.

Do NOT make the site feel like a lead-generation site or “quiz funnel.” Buyers should feel like they are shopping intelligently, not being sold as leads.

Maintain all existing flows unless absolutely necessary:

* `/match`
* `/browse`
* `/outfitter`
* `/pricing`
* `/sell`
* `/trips`
* `/dealers/login`

Preserve existing backend integrations and APIs whenever possible.

---

# HIGH-LEVEL STRATEGY

The homepage hierarchy should shift from:

1. Match Report
2. AI tools
3. Marketplace

TO:

1. Browse marketplace inventory
2. Match Report / AI guidance
3. Additional tools

The website should visually and structurally resemble a modern trusted marketplace similar to:

* Zillow
* AutoTrader
* CarGurus
* RVTrader
* Redfin

NOT a startup landing page or lead-generation funnel.

The experience should feel:

* inventory-first,
* trustworthy,
* practical,
* premium,
* mobile-friendly,
* and easy to understand instantly.

Avoid overly clever marketing copy that sacrifices clarity.
