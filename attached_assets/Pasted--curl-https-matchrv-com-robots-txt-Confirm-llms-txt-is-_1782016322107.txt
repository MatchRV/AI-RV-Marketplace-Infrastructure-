# MatchRV

> MatchRV is an AI-powered RV marketplace based in the Pacific Northwest. It helps buyers find the right RV through a personalized AI Match Report, and connects them with live inventory from Washington State dealers.

## About MatchRV

MatchRV was founded by Jonathan Kitchel, who spent 8 years on the floor at Poulsbo RV — one of the largest RV dealerships in the United States. He built MatchRV to solve the problems he saw firsthand: buyers overwhelmed by choices, dealers with disconnected inventory tools, and no neutral platform that genuinely put the buyer first.

MatchRV currently indexes 4,000+ live RV listings from Washington State dealers, updated daily. Every listing is scored against market value to surface genuine deals.

## Key Features

- **AI Match Report**: Free personalized report that narrows thousands of listings to three specific RVs — a Best Overall, Best Value, and Upgrade — based on the buyer's lifestyle, budget, and tow vehicle.
- **Live Inventory**: 4,000+ RV listings from Washington State dealers, updated daily via automated scraping.
- **Deal Scoring**: Every listing is compared against estimated market value. Listings are rated Great Deal, Good Deal, Fair Price, or Overpriced.
- **Tow Guide**: Free tool to check whether a specific RV can be safely towed by the user's vehicle.
- **Trip Planner**: AI-powered tool to plan RV trips with routes, campgrounds, and driving legs.
- **Outfitter Chat**: AI assistant that answers RV questions and helps narrow down choices.

## RV Types Covered

Travel Trailers, Fifth Wheels, Class A Motorhomes, Class B Motorhomes (Camper Vans), Class C Motorhomes, Toy Haulers, Pop-Up Campers, Truck Campers.
