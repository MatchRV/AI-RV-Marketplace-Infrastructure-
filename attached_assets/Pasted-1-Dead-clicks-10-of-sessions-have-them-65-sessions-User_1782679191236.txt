1. Dead clicks — ~10% of sessions have them (65 sessions)
Users are clicking on non-interactive elements on listing pages and forms — buttons or areas that look clickable but do nothing

Clarity flagged dead clicks at 10.37% of sessions (65 total) — this is a real UX problem

Specifically called out: elements on listing pages and the match report form area

Fix: Make non-interactive hero text (like the headline "Find the right RV without wasting months researching") visually distinct from clickable elements

2. Quick backs — 6.86% of sessions (43 sessions)
Users are hitting a page and immediately going back — this is a bounce indicator

Happening on listing pages and after some form interactions

Fix: Review what users see on first landing — likely the listing detail pages aren't meeting expectations set by the listing card

3. Broken/outdated URLs causing 404 errors
Multiple sessions showed users hitting /about-us and /fleet — pages that don't exist anymore — resulting in "Page Not Found" errors

These are likely coming from old marketing links, social posts, or backlinks

Fix: Set up 301 redirects from /about-us → /about and /fleet → /browse (or wherever appropriate)

4. Dealer login is failing to convert
Dealer login sessions were initiated but not completed — users enter info and stop before signing in

Fix: Check the dealer login flow for friction (confusing fields, unclear error messages, or a required step that's blocking them)

5. Mobile scroll drop-off on the homepage
Mobile users had steep drop-off after just 10% scroll depth on the homepage

Only ~55% of mobile users reach the 10% scroll mark — which means most are NOT seeing your inventory or the Match CTA below the fold

Fix: Surface the "Get My Free Match Report" CTA above the fold on mobile, or make the hero section shorter

6. CLS (Cumulative Layout Shift) is POOR — score 3.5
This means page elements are jumping around during load, which frustrates users and hurts SEO

Fix: This is likely caused by images loading without defined dimensions, or dynamic content injecting into the layout (ads, chat widget, etc.)

7. Tablet experience is weak
Tablet users showed minimal scroll behavior and half their clicks concentrated only on "Get My Free Match Report" — they're not exploring further

Tablet interactions on the Browse page were minimal compared to desktop/mobile

Fix: Test the tablet layout specifically — filter sidebar and listing cards likely don't render well at tablet widths

8. The Match page has dead clicks on the main content container
Users are clicking on the non-interactive page body/text area around the chat widget — likely trying to interact with something that isn't clickable

Fix: Make the chat input field more visually prominent as the main action area

9. Very low returning user rate (8.93%)
Only 56 of 627 sessions are returning users — 91% are brand new

This suggests people are finding you, checking it out, and not coming back

Fix: Consider email capture after a Match Report submission, or push notifications for new listings matching their criteria
