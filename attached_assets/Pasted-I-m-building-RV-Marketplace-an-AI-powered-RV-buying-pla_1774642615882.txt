I'm building RV Marketplace — an AI-powered RV buying platform (think CarGurus for RVs). 

My tech stack is: Next.js 14, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Supabase (PostgreSQL), Stripe for payments, Resend for email.

I need you to build two connected features: Ghost Listings and the Dealer Claim Flow. Here is exactly what each does:

---

## FEATURE 1: GHOST LISTINGS

### What it is
Every RV dealership in America gets a profile page on my platform automatically — even if they never signed up. These are called "ghost listings." The data comes from a CSV I will upload (dealer name, address, phone, city, state, zip, website, specialties).

### Database
Add a `dealers` table in Supabase with these columns:
- id (uuid, primary key)
- name (text)
- address (text)
- city (text)
- state (text)
- zip (text)
- phone (text)
- website (text)
- specialties (text) — e.g. "Class C, Travel Trailers"
- status (enum: 'ghost', 'claimed', 'active') — default 'ghost'
- claimed_by_user_id (uuid, nullable, foreign key to auth.users)
- claimed_at (timestamp, nullable)
- trial_ends_at (timestamp, nullable)
- stripe_customer_id (text, nullable)
- stripe_subscription_id (text, nullable)
- created_at (timestamp)

### Admin Import Page
Build a page at /admin/import-dealers where I can upload a CSV and it bulk-inserts all dealers with status = 'ghost'.

### Public Dealer Profile Page
Build a page at /dealers/[id] that shows:
- Dealer name, address, phone, specialties
- A yellow banner at the top that says: "⚠️ This dealer hasn't joined RV Marketplace yet. They can't receive messages until they claim this listing."
- A button: "Are you this dealer? Claim your free listing →" that links to /claim/[id]
- If status is 'claimed' or 'active', hide the banner and show normal profile

## FEATURE 2: BUYER INQUIRY + DEALER CLAIM FLOW

### Step 1 — Buyer Sends Inquiry to Ghost Dealer
When a buyer gets matched to a ghost dealer (status = 'ghost'), show them this UI:

> "Dave's RV Center was matched to you by our AI — but they haven't joined RV Marketplace yet."
> [Send them your inquiry anyway — we'll notify them] ← button

When buyer clicks that button, save an `inquiries` table row:
- id (uuid)
- dealer_id (foreign key to dealers)
- buyer_name (text)
- buyer_email (text)
- buyer_phone (text, optional)
- budget (text)
- rv_type (text)
- timeline (text)
- tow_vehicle (text, optional)
- message (text, optional)
- status (enum: 'pending', 'delivered', 'responded')
- created_at (timestamp)

Then immediately send an email to the dealer's listed email address using Resend. The email looks like this:

Subject: "A buyer is waiting for you on RV Marketplace"

Body:
---
Hi [Dealer Name],

A buyer was matched to your dealership by our AI today.

Here are their details:
• Name: [buyer name]
• Budget: [budget]
• Looking for: [rv type]
• Timeline: [timeline]
• Tow vehicle: [tow vehicle]

They're waiting for a response.

[Claim Your Free Listing and Respond →] ← big button linking to /claim/[dealer_id]?inquiry=[inquiry_id]

This takes 60 seconds. Free for 30 days. No credit card required to start.

— The RV Marketplace Team
---

### Step 2 — Dealer Claim Flow (/claim/[dealer_id])
This is a 3-step wizard:

STEP 1 — Verify Identity
- Show dealer name and address
- Form fields: Your name, Your email, Your phone, Your role at dealership (Owner / Manager / Sales)
- Button: "This is my dealership — continue"

STEP 2 — Confirm Your Info
- Pre-filled from ghost listing data (editable): dealership name, address, phone, website, specialties
- Upload logo (optional)
- Button: "Looks good — activate my free trial"

STEP 3 — Confirmation
- Show: "You're live! Your 30-day free trial has started."
- If there is a pending inquiry (passed via URL param), show it immediately:
  > "You have a buyer waiting. Here's their inquiry: [show inquiry details]"
  > [Reply to this buyer →] ← opens email compose or shows buyer contact info
- Show link to their new dealer dashboard: /dashboard/dealer

### After Claim
- Set dealer status = 'claimed'
- Set trial_ends_at = now + 30 days
- Set claimed_by_user_id = auth user id (create account for them if needed using their email)
- Update inquiry status = 'delivered'
