## Replit prompt

You are a senior product designer, technical SEO specialist, CRO expert, and full-stack frontend engineer. Audit and rebuild LotLink.io so it feels like a 9.9/10 premium marketplace brand, loads fast, ranks well, converts better, and clearly beats competing RV listing sites in both search visibility and monthly visitors.

Your mission:
- Fix the current website weaknesses.
- Keep the brand name LotLink.
- Make the site look elite, trustworthy, modern, and conversion-focused.
- Optimize for SEO, organic traffic, direct traffic, and lead generation.
- Build improvements that give LotLink a clear edge over RV Trader, Facebook Marketplace listings, dealer websites, and generic RV directories.

Context about current problems to fix:
- The value proposition is not yet sharp enough; users should instantly understand why LotLink is better within 3 seconds.[1]
- The site appears early-stage / pre-launch, so it needs more trust, more proof, more structure, and stronger conversion paths.[1]
- Messaging is likely too feature-led and not outcome-led; shift copy toward “find the right RV faster, compare smarter, and connect with real inventory.”[1]
- The site needs stronger information architecture for SEO, including crawlable category pages, location pages, buyer guides, comparison content, and structured internal linking.[1]
- It likely over-relies on a landing-page style experience instead of a scalable marketplace content engine, which limits SEO and monthly visitor growth.[1]
- It needs stronger EEAT signals: transparent company story, expert content, dealer credibility, FAQs, trust badges, privacy clarity, and real shopper/dealer proof.[1]
- It needs better technical SEO, Core Web Vitals, schema, metadata, image optimization, and semantic HTML to compete at scale.[1]

Deliverables:
1. A full homepage rebuild.
2. A complete new sitemap and SEO architecture.
3. High-converting copy for the homepage.
4. UI/UX improvements that make the site feel premium and easier to use.
5. Technical SEO fixes.
6. CRO fixes to increase signup, waitlist, lead form, and inventory engagement.
7. Content strategy to grow monthly visitors.
8. A competitor-advantage strategy.
9. Production-ready code changes, not just recommendations.
10. A final changelog explaining what was improved and why.

Execution rules:
- Do not give generic advice.
- Do not use filler copy like “empowering your journey” or “all-in-one solution.”
- Write specific, sharp, believable copy.
- Make every section serve SEO, conversion, trust, or product clarity.
- Prioritize speed, clarity, and authority.
- Use semantic HTML, accessible components, strong heading hierarchy, and mobile-first responsive design.
- Design should feel like a premium marketplace startup, not a template.
- Make the site visually cleaner, more confident, and more credible than typical RV websites.
- Avoid cliché SaaS gradients and generic AI aesthetics.
- Use strong typography, clean spacing, clear CTA hierarchy, and trust-building sections.

Homepage requirements:
- Above the fold must clearly answer:
  - What is LotLink?
  - Who is it for?
  - Why is it better?
  - What should the visitor do next?
- Include:
  - Strong headline and subheadline
  - Primary CTA and secondary CTA
  - Trust/credibility strip
  - “How it works” section
  - Benefits for shoppers
  - Benefits for dealers
  - Featured inventory preview
  - AI assistant explanation
  - Comparison table: LotLink vs traditional RV shopping
  - SEO-rich FAQ section
  - Final CTA
- Add stronger social proof placeholders that can later be replaced with real metrics, testimonials, and logos.
- Add a section explaining real-time inventory aggregation and why that matters.
- Add a section that reduces buyer anxiety: stale listings, hidden pricing, too many sites, poor filters, wasted time.
- Add a dealer growth section focused on better leads, fresher exposure, and less dependence on outdated listing models.

Create a scalable sitemap for:

For each page type, generate:
- SEO title formula
- Meta description formula

- Schema type
- Recommended content blocks
- Conversion CTA

Technical SEO requirements:
- Improve Core Web Vitals.
- Ensure proper title tags and meta descriptions.
- Add canonical tags.
- Add Open Graph and Twitter card tags.
- Add JSON-LD schema for Organization, WebSite, SearchAction, FAQPage, Product/Vehicle where relevant, and BreadcrumbList.
- Improve heading structure.
- Improve semantic HTML.
- Compress/lazy-load media.
- Use descriptive alt text.
- Generate robots.txt recommendations.
- Generate sitemap.xml recommendations.
- Add internal linking modules throughout the site.
- Ensure pages are indexable and crawl-friendly.
- Build pages to rank for transactional, informational, and comparison intent.

CRO requirements:
- Improve CTA placement and clarity.
- Reduce friction in signup/waitlist forms.
- Add trust-building microcopy near forms.
- Add comparison and objection-handling sections.
- Add sticky mobile CTA.
- Add stronger dealer lead capture.
- Improve browse-to-lead flow.
- Add email capture hooks for buyers not ready to convert.

Content strategy requirements:
Create a traffic plan that helps LotLink beat competitors over time:
- Programmatic SEO for city/state/category pages
- Comparison pages
- Buyer guides
- Dealer pages
- Seasonal trend content
- Used vs new RV content
- Family, towing, boondocking, luxury, and full-time RV living clusters

Need from you:
- First, audit the current site structure and identify design, UX, copy, trust, SEO, speed, and conversion weaknesses.
- Then produce a prioritized execution roadmap:
  - Quick wins in 1 day
  - High-impact fixes in 1 week
  - Growth moat work in 30-60 days
- Then implement the redesigned homepage and supporting SEO structure in code.
- Then provide a written explanation of how these changes improve rankings, visitors, trust, and conversions.

Output format:
1. Audit findings
2. Priority roadmap
3. New sitemap and SEO architecture
4. Revised homepage copy
5. UI/UX upgrade plan
6. Technical SEO fixes

10. Changelog with business impact

Success standard:
When finished, the site should feel credible enough for investors, polished enough for premium dealers, simple enough for first-time RV buyers, and structured well enough to become an SEO machine.

## Notes to tailor it
A stronger version is to add your real stack at the top, for example:
- “Frontend is Next.js”
- “Backend/database is Supabase”
- “Current deployment is on Replit”
- “Do not break existing auth, forms, or analytics”

That makes Replit produce more usable code on the first pass.

## Best add-on
Append this line at the end of the prompt:

“Before coding, ask me for the current file tree and then make changes file-by-file with exact replacement code.”

That forces a more controlled refactor and usually gets better results.[1]
