Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about selling on MatchRV.

What does it cost to list my RV? expand_more
Standard listings are completely free for all sellers. We offer optional premium placement packages for owners of ultra-high-end assets who want maximum visibility across our partner network.
How long does a typical sale take? expand_more
While individual results vary, our platform average for a luxury RV is roughly 14-21 days from listing to accepted offer. Highly sought-after models often move within the first week.

Yes. MatchRV uses bank-level encryption for all messages and data transfers. We never share your personal contact information with potential buyers until you choose to reveal it.

Absolutely. We provide buyers with instant access to our network of preferred lenders, making it easier for them to secure funding for your RV, which facilitates a faster sale for you.
